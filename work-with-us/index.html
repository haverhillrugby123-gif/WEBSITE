Tutor standards

What we value in a tutor.

Strong subject knowledge matters, but so do explanation, diagnosis, feedback, adaptation and professional communication. Applicants should be ready to discuss relevant qualifications, experience and safeguarding checks. Parents can ask for the relevant position for any proposed tutor to be confirmed before lessons.

What matters

Clear teaching and professional judgement.

Strong subject knowledge matters, but so does the ability to diagnose misconceptions, explain ideas in more than one way, give useful feedback and adapt to the student in front of you.

Explain rather than simply supply answers
Use student work to guide next steps
Communicate clearly and professionally
Keep claims about experience and outcomes accurate
