The approach

Start with what the pupil can do.

A recent answer, a school task or a few targeted questions can reveal more than a broad label such as “struggling with English”. The aim is to identify the specific gap and make the next task useful.

Clear modelling shows how to approach a question. Guided practice gives the pupil a chance to try. Feedback then identifies what to keep, what to change and where to focus next.

What matters in a lesson.

01

A specific priority

Work on a misconception, skill or exam habit that is holding the pupil back.

02

Thinking made visible

Explain why an approach works, so the pupil can use it in a new question.

03

Growing independence

Reduce prompts as understanding becomes more secure, and use the work to judge progress.

Choosing a tutor

The right experience for your child.

The founder’s background is one part of the service. Before arranging tuition, discuss the proposed tutor’s qualifications, experience in the subject and stage, teaching approach and relevant safeguarding checks.

Availability, lesson format, fees and cancellation arrangements should also be clear before you decide.
