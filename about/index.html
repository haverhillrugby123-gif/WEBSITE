About UK Online Tuition

Tuition should be specific enough to change what happens next.

UK Online Tuition provides personalised online support for GCSE, 11+ and Primary pupils. The focus is not on selling a fixed package. It is on diagnosing the student’s current needs, teaching clearly and using their work to decide the next useful step.

How we think about tuition

A small set of principles guides the work.

In practiceStart with evidence

Use a recent answer, school task or targeted question to choose the real priority.

01

Start with evidence

Current work and questioning are more useful than assumptions about what a student “should” already know.

02

Make thinking visible

Good modelling explains the decisions behind an answer so pupils can transfer the method to a new task.

03

Do not confuse activity with progress

Completing more pages is not the goal. The useful question is what the pupil can now understand or do more securely.

04

Build independence

Support should reduce as competence grows. Strong tuition ultimately makes the student less dependent on prompting.

What this means for a pupil

Professional judgement should shape the next lesson.

Plans matter, but they should respond to what the student actually demonstrates. A misconception discovered today may be more important than the topic originally pencilled in for next week.
