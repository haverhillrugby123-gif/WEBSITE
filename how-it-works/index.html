How it works

From your first question

Discuss the support, tutor and practical arrangements before deciding to start.

Start an enquiry ↗
  1. 01 / Enquire

    Tell us what would help.

    Share the year group, subject or entrance test, and the main difficulty or goal. You do not need to know exactly what the problem is.

    Use the enquiry email builder or email us directly. Review and send the message from your email service.

  2. 02 / Discuss

    Make an informed choice.

    Discuss the proposed tutor’s experience, your child’s needs and suitable lesson times.

    Confirm the current fee, lesson format and cancellation terms before making arrangements.

  3. 03 / Begin

    Agree the first priority.

    If the support is suitable, arrange an online lesson. Current school work or a recent assessment can help identify a starting point.

    The plan develops as the tutor sees what the pupil understands and can do independently.

Online lessons via Zoom

A little preparation makes a difference.

Lessons may include shared work, whiteboards, worked examples, discussion and independent practice.

Before the first lesson

  • Check that Zoom works on your device.
  • Choose a reliable connection and a quiet place to work.
  • Use a laptop or larger screen where possible.
  • Have relevant questions, school work and writing materials ready.
  • Confirm the time and joining instructions with your tutor.

During and after lessons

Look for progress in the work.

The tutor explains and models an approach, then gives the pupil a chance to use it. Questions and feedback help identify what needs more practice.

Useful signs of progress include more accurate answers, clearer explanations and less reliance on prompts. Discuss priorities and next steps with the tutor as lessons continue.
