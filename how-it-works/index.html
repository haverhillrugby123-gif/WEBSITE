How online tuition works

A clear process before, during and after each lesson.

Parents should know what they are buying. The process starts with the student’s current needs, turns those into specific teaching priorities, and uses subsequent work to decide what comes next.

From enquiry to purposeful tuition.

01

Tell us the situation

Year group, subject or entrance test, current difficulty and what you would most like to improve.

02

Clarify the priority

Where useful, current work or recent assessment evidence helps identify the most important starting point.

03

Teach and practise

Lessons combine explanation, modelling, questioning and practice rather than simply supervising worksheets.

04

Use the evidence

What the pupil can and cannot yet do informs the next target. The plan can change when the evidence changes.

Inside an online lesson

Active teaching, not passive screen time.

Online tuition works best when the student is doing substantial thinking. A lesson may use shared documents, screen sharing, whiteboards, exam questions, uploaded school work or short diagnostic tasks depending on the subject.

The tutor can model a method live, ask the student to explain decisions, annotate work together and then reduce support as the pupil becomes more secure.

What parents should expect

Clarity without constant sales promises.

Relevant tutor information

A clear lesson purpose

The lesson should have a reason beyond simply filling an hour.

Progress visible in the work

Look for changes in accuracy, independence, method, explanation or exam performance rather than vague reassurance alone.

Flexibility when the evidence changes

A useful tuition plan adapts when new school work, mocks or lesson performance reveal a different priority.
