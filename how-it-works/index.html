Online lessons via Zoom

A little preparation makes a difference.

Lessons may include shared work, whiteboards, worked examples, discussion and independent practice.

Before the first lesson

  • Check that Zoom works on your device.
  • Choose a reliable connection and a quiet place to work.
  • Use a laptop or larger screen where possible.
  • Have relevant questions, school work and writing materials ready.
  • Confirm the time and joining instructions with your tutor.

During and after lessons

Look for progress in the work.

The tutor explains and models an approach, then gives the pupil a chance to use it. Questions and feedback help identify what needs more practice.

Useful signs of progress include more accurate answers, clearer explanations and less reliance on prompts. Discuss priorities and next steps with the tutor as lessons continue.

You can cancel a lesson with at least 24 hours’ notice. If you give less than 24 hours’ notice, the lesson is chargeable.

Maths · equivalent fractions

One idea. Three steps.

  1. 1See it2/4 = 1/2. Divide both numbers by 2.
  2. 2Try itCan you simplify 4/8 using the same idea?
  3. 3Explain itWhy does the amount stay the same?
See the explanation

4/8 = 1/2. Dividing the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number keeps the fraction equivalent.
