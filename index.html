Practical questions.
Clear answers.

All frequently asked questions →
How much does tuition cost?

Fees depend on the tutor, subject, level and lesson format. Include the year group and subject in your enquiry and ask for the current fee before arranging lessons.

What do we need for online lessons?

A reliable internet connection and a device that can run Zoom. A laptop or larger screen helps when reading texts, questions and shared work.

Is tuition one-to-one?

One-to-one or small-group tuition may be available where suitable. The right format depends on the pupil, subject and current availability.

What happens when I enquire?

The enquiry form helps you prepare an email to review and send. You can also email us directly. An enquiry lets you discuss support; it does not book or pay for a lesson.
