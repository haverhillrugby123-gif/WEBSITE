Is every 11+ exam the same?

No. The format can vary by area, consortium and school. Current official admissions information should determine the plan.

When should preparation become timed?

Once core skills are reasonably secure, timed and format-specific practice becomes more useful. Doing timed papers too early can simply rehearse the same gaps.

Can tuition guarantee an offer?

No responsible tutor can guarantee a selective-school place. Tuition can improve preparation, knowledge, familiarity and confidence; admissions outcomes depend on the assessment and wider process.

The teaching cycle

Preparation should become more specific over time.

Early work can build foundations. Closer to the test, practice should increasingly reflect the real format, timing and demands.

01

Diagnose

Use current work, questioning and short tasks to find the real barrier.

02

Model

Make the hidden thinking visible: explain why a method works, not only what to write.

03

Practise

Move from supported examples to independent application and unfamiliar questions.

04

Respond to evidence

Use errors and successful work to decide what the pupil should do next.

A simple standard

A student should finish knowing something they did not know, or doing something they could not do, at the start.

That is a more useful measure of a lesson than how many pages were completed.
