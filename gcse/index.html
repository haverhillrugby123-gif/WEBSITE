Do you teach different exam boards?

The exact tutor and specification should be confirmed before lessons. Paper-specific teaching should always use the student’s current exam board rather than a generic GCSE template.

Can tuition focus on one paper or one weak area?

Yes. A diagnostic approach can focus on a specific paper, topic, text or recurring skill when that is the highest-value priority.

How many lessons will a student need?

There is no responsible universal number. Current attainment, gaps, goals, time available and independent practice all change the answer.

The teaching cycle

Good GCSE tuition is not just more questions.

Practice matters, but only when it is aimed at the right misconception, method or exam habit.

01

Diagnose

Use current work, questioning and short tasks to find the real barrier.

02

Model

Make the hidden thinking visible: explain why a method works, not only what to write.

03

Practise

Move from supported examples to independent application and unfamiliar questions.

04

Respond to evidence

Use errors and successful work to decide what the pupil should do next.

Why it mattersDiagnose

A simple standard

A student should finish knowing something they did not know, or doing something they could not do, at the start.

That is a more useful measure of a lesson than how many pages were completed.
