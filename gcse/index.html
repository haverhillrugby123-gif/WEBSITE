What we can work on

Support that gets to the specific difficulty

English

Comprehension, analysis and writing

Work on interpreting questions, using evidence and developing a clear, controlled response.

Maths

Understanding as well as method

Revisit missing knowledge, explain each step and practise applying it to unfamiliar questions.

Science

Concepts, calculations and application

Connect scientific ideas with the calculations and exam questions that test them.

The teaching approach

From a difficult question to an independent answer

  1. 01

    Find the gap

    Use current work and short questions to identify the topic or exam habit that needs attention.

  2. 02

    Explain the method

    Work through an example, including why each decision helps the answer.

  3. 03

    Try it independently

    Apply the method to a fresh question, with less support as understanding develops.

  4. 04

    Review and refine

    Use the answer to choose the next topic, method or revision priority.

Useful progress means a student can explain or apply something they found difficult before the lesson.

Before you enquire

A few useful answers

Can tuition focus on one topic or paper?

Yes. Support can focus on a particular topic, text, paper or recurring skill when that is the most useful priority.

Which exam boards are covered?

Confirm the tutor and specification before lessons. Tell us the student’s exam board and tier, if known, in your enquiry.

How many lessons are needed?

This depends on current understanding, goals, time available and independent practice. The first discussion should establish a sensible starting point.
