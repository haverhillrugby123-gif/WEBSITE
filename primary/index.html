The teaching approach

Help your child do more of the thinking

  1. 01

    Find the starting point

    Use a short task and conversation to see what is secure and what needs attention.

  2. 02

    Show and explain

    Model a manageable example, making the thinking behind it clear.

  3. 03

    Practise with purpose

    Try related tasks and gradually reduce prompts as understanding grows.

  4. 04

    Choose the next step

    Use the work to decide what to revisit, consolidate or extend.

Confidence can grow from real understanding: being able to tackle a task with less help matters more than completing more worksheets.

Before you enquire

A few useful answers

Will tuition mean more worksheets?

Tasks should have a clear purpose: revealing a gap, practising a skill or applying an idea. Explanation, discussion and feedback are part of the work.

Can tuition provide more challenge?

Yes. Tell us what your child already finds straightforward and where they would benefit from deeper thinking or a new challenge.

Can you help with Year 6 and transition?

Support can cover SATs preparation where relevant, alongside the reading, number and independent working skills needed for secondary school.
