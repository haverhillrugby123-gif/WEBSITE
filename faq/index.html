Practical questions. Clear answers.
How much does tuition cost?
Fees vary depending on the tutor’s experience. Ask for the current fee before arranging lessons.
Do you offer a free initial consultation?
Daniel offers a free initial consultation to understand what you are looking for, discuss your child’s needs and help identify suitable tuition support.
Can I request a bespoke tuition package?
Individual tuition and bespoke packages are welcome. Discuss your child’s priorities with Daniel so you can agree suitable support and arrangements.
How long is a lesson?
Lessons last between 30 minutes and two hours. Agree the length before arranging your lesson.
Who teaches the lessons?
Founder Daniel Harris teaches lessons. Discuss your child’s subject, stage and support needs with Daniel before arranging tuition.
When do I pay?
Payment is due after each lesson.
Do you offer one-to-one lessons and groups?
UK Online Tuition provides personalised support and can offer one-to-one or small-group tuition where suitable. The right format depends on the student, subject and current availability.
How does online tuition work?
Lessons take place online via Zoom. The exact lesson format depends on the student and subject, but may include screen sharing, digital whiteboards, worked examples, discussion and independent practice.
What technology do I need?
A reliable internet connection and a device that can run Zoom are the essentials. A larger screen is usually more comfortable for viewing texts, questions and shared work than a phone.
What qualifications or experience will my tutor have?
Ask us about the proposed tutor’s qualifications and experience in your child’s subject and level. You can also discuss their teaching approach before deciding whether the tuition is suitable.
Can I cancel a lesson?
You can cancel a lesson with at least 24 hours’ notice. If you give less than 24 hours’ notice, the lesson is chargeable.
What should I include in an enquiry?
The most useful starting information is the student’s year group, subject, current level where known, and the main area you want help with. That makes it easier to judge what support is appropriate. Use the enquiry form to share the year group, subject and support needed. You can also prepare an optional email to review and send through your email service. An enquiry does not book or pay for a lesson.
What if the tutor is not the right fit?
Contact us and explain what is not working. Teaching style and pupil-tutor fit matter, and the sensible next step is to review the situation and discuss the available alternatives.
Can lessons help with exam technique as well as subject knowledge?
Yes. For exam courses, technique is most useful when it sits alongside secure subject understanding. Lessons can therefore combine knowledge, modelling, timed or exam-style practice and feedback.
How are lessons planned?
Strong tuition should respond to evidence. A tutor may start with a planned topic, but current work, questioning and misconceptions should influence what is taught next and how much support the student needs.
